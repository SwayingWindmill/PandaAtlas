# Archived research scripts

This directory is for historical one-off scripts retained only to reproduce or audit already-frozen research evidence.

Archive by year or evidence package. Each archived group should identify:

- the reviewed output or evidence it produced;
- source inputs and required parameters;
- the commit or batch where it was last used;
- whether a reusable replacement exists;
- conditions under which the script may be deleted.

Archived scripts are exempt from round/date filename restrictions, but they are not supported entry points and must not receive new product or research behavior.
