## Approved direction

- Pose: low, resting giant panda with the head turned toward the viewer.
- Rear body: continuous low mass with **no white rear-leg cutout**, avoiding the raised-rump reading rejected during review.
- Style: monochrome, minimal, quiet, non-mascot, photography-compatible.
- No forced `Z` or bamboo motif.
- Primary production color should inherit the ZhiPanda deep teal / ivory system rather than baking color into the mark.
